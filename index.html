🔴
Peak hours (weekdays) — your 5-hour session limits are consumed faster than normal. Affects free, Pro, and Max plans. ~7% of Pro users will hit limits they normally wouldn't.
✅
Off-peak hours & weekends — session limits deplete at the standard rate. Best time for token-intensive or background jobs.
📊
Weekly totals unchanged — only the distribution across the week changes. Shifting heavy work to off-peak stretches your limits further.
💾
